## What we did not manage

- Not done at the time of this draft (all needed before tagging):
  - the ≤ 5-minute recording;
  - the CI run for the `pa3` tag;
  - the five passwords and the tag commit in `PA3.md`;
  - a dated screenshot of the AI Studio cap.
- The first deployment in Azure `eastasia` (Hong Kong) could not call Gemini at all ("User location is not supported"). We found this only when we turned the model on, and moved to Japan East.
- On 24 Nov the model cited every evidence line one too low on the deployment. Strict evidence validation turned right verdicts into `unsure`. We fixed it by sending numbered lines (PR #22). Before that fix, the committed run reached only 56.8% recall.
- Two violations are still missed on the dev set: `dynamic-array/s05 C2` (unsure) and `dynamic-array/s07 C3` (predicted pass).
- We have not run attacks of our own against the deployment beyond the CI attack tests.
- The commit history of 21 Nov does not show the parts being built one after another in real time.
- Many AI-LOG entries have no real Rejected item.

## What we would do differently

- Turn the model on against the real deployment on day one. Both the region block and the line-number drift were invisible with `MODEL_OFF=1` and with fake-model tests.
- Commit each part as it is finished, test first, instead of committing several parts at the end of a session.
- Write AI-LOG Changed / Rejected lines at the moment something is thrown away, not afterwards.
- Run the marker's flow through the browser, with the exact accounts, as soon as the deployment exists. The "earlier submission disappears" problem only showed up when one student submitted twice.
